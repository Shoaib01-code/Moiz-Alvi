01The Studio

We photograph weddings the way they feel — unhurried, honest and full of quiet light. From the first dholki to the final rukhsati, every frame is made to be kept, not just seen.

M.A Production is a photography studio on Model Town Link Road, Lahore, led by Moiz Alvi. Weddings are at the heart of what we do — with fashion, food, corporate and studio work under the same roof.

No stiff poses, no rushing. Just your people, your day, and photographs that still feel true twenty years from now.
